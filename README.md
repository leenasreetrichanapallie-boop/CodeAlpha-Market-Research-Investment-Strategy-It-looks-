# Market Research & Investment Strategy – CodeAlpha Internship

This project is submitted as part of the CodeAlpha Finance & Investment Analysis Internship.

## Task 4 – Mini Project

The objective of this project is to analyze investment trends, compare stock markets and cryptocurrencies, understand risk management strategies, and study the future outlook of different investment sectors.

## Topics Covered
- Stock Market vs Cryptocurrency Trends
- Risk Management in Investments
- Diversified Investment Strategies
- Future Outlook of Investment Sectors
- Investment Opportunities & Risks

## Analysis Includes
- Comparative market analysis
- Investment strategy research
- Risk management techniques
- Sector growth analysis
- Charts & graphical representation
- Conclusion and references

## Charts Included
- Stock Market vs Cryptocurrency Risk Comparison Bar Graph
- Diversified Investment Portfolio Pie Chart
- Future Growth Potential Column Chart

## Tools Used
- Microsoft Word
- Google Sheets
- GitHub

## Key Learning Outcomes
- Understanding financial markets and investment trends
- Comparing traditional and digital investments
- Learning risk management strategies
- Analyzing future investment opportunities
- Creating professional financial research reports

## Project Outcome
This project helped in understanding how market trends, diversification, and risk management influence investment decisions. It also provided insights into future growth sectors such as technology, renewable energy, electric vehicles, and digital finance.
